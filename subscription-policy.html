Subscription Policy

What Plus and Premium actually do — and what they don't promise.

This is a hobby project, not a company, and this isn't written by a lawyer. It's an honest explanation of what buying a subscription actually gets you.

These are not real-money subscriptions

Plus and Premium are paid for entirely with Chum Coins — the in-game currency you earn by fishing. No real money, credit card, or payment processor is involved anywhere in this game right now. Chum Coins have no cash value and can't be exchanged for real currency.

What the daily usage limit actually is

Every free player gets 100 cloud "reads" and 100 cloud "writes" per day — roughly, 100 times the game can check something from the server (like a leaderboard) and 100 times it can save something to the server (like a new catch or a trade). This resets every day at midnight.

This limit exists to keep hosting costs sustainable so the game can stay free for everyone. It is not a security feature and isn't meant to be one.

What Plus and Premium change

  • Plus: 2x the daily reads and writes (200/200), plus a badge next to your name wherever it appears
  • Premium: 10x the daily reads and writes (1,000/1,000), the badge, and priority access to try new features before they're released to everyone

That's the whole promise. A subscription makes the ceiling higher — it doesn't change how fishing, catching, or scoring works, and it doesn't give any competitive advantage in gameplay itself.

What it doesn't promise

  • It doesn't guarantee you'll never see a limit message — extremely heavy use on any tier could still reach a (much higher) ceiling
  • It doesn't guarantee uptime, feature availability, or that "early access" features will ship on any particular schedule
  • It isn't a security or account-protection product

Refunds

Since this is paid for with in-game currency rather than real money, purchases are treated as final — there's no refund system built for Chum Coin purchases. If something clearly went wrong (a bug charged you twice, for example), reach out and it can be sorted out manually.

Expiration

Subscriptions run for the exact length you purchase (1 day, 1 month, or 1 year) and expire automatically — there's no auto-renewal. Buying a new plan while one is already active replaces it rather than stacking on top of it.

If this ever changes

If a real-money option is ever added in the future, or these terms change in any meaningful way, this page will be updated to reflect that before it happens.

Last updated: alongside Driftline's Plus/Premium subscription launch.
