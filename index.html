    Driftline

    Nearby docks

    Real piers, marinas, beaches and tackle shops near you, plus drift buoys. Walk within 120 m of one to visit it for free bait, and sometimes an egg.

    Hatchery

    Eggs come from docks. Your first three eggs incubate as you walk; when one has been carried far enough, tap it to hatch a fish worth double points.

    Crews

    Every dock can be held by one of three crews. Visiting a dock strengthens your crew's hold on it, or wears down a rival's until you take it over. Fishing within 150 m of a dock your crew holds gives you +5% bite chance.
